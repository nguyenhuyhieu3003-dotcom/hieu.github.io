Introduce

T-shirts are one of the most important pieces of clothing in everyone's wardrobe. A suitable shirt provides comfort, excellent sweat absorption, and the confidence to express your personal style. Different purposes and weather conditions require different types of fabrics.

Common types of fabrics.

  • 100% Cotton: Breathable, excellent sweat absorption, suitable for dry and hot weather.
  • Polyester (Poly): Durable, wrinkle-resistant, quick-drying, often used for sportswear.
  • CVC (65/35 Cotton): The perfect combination of cotton's softness and polyester's shape retention.
  • Linen: Very light and cool, designed for outdoor activities or hot summer days.
